The essentials

Four patterns worth remembering.

Urgency & fear

Scammers may claim an account will close or a fine is due immediately. Pause and verify through a trusted channel you find independently.

Signal 01

Credential requests

Do not share passwords, one-time codes, recovery phrases, or private keys with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

Signal 02

Unexpected links

Inspect the actual hostname, not just the visible brand. Avoid unknown attachments and downloads when unsure.

Signal 03

Unusual payments

Requests for gift cards, cryptocurrency, secret transfers, or fees to unlock a prize deserve extra scrutiny.

Signal 04

Impersonation

A logo or familiar name is easy to copy. Verify identity with an official app, saved contact, or independently sourced number.

Signal 05

Security bypass

Be cautious if someone asks you to disable protection, install remote-control software, or ignore a browser warning.

Signal 06
A calm response

Stop. Check. Verify. Report.

Never use a link or phone number in a suspicious message to verify that same message.

Quick checklist

Before you click.

  • Did you expect this message?
  • Is the sender verified independently?
  • Does it demand secrecy or immediate action?
  • Is it asking for credentials, money, or remote access?
  • Can you reach the organization through its official app?

02 — Keep codes and passwords private.

03 — Verify using a known-good channel.
