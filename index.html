01 / The mission

Make the warning signs make sense.

Scam messages often use pressure, secrecy, credential requests, or unusual payment methods. Learn how to spot combinations of signals without treating a score as a verdict.

This educational tool uses local heuristics, not live threat-intelligence feeds or a cloud AI model. A high score is not proof; a low score is not a safety guarantee.
