IMPORTANT LIMITATION

Can this prove an image is AI-generated or real?

Not reliably yet. The project includes a training pipeline, but no trained weights or labeled dataset are bundled. Metadata can be removed or edited, and AI-generated images can contain camera-like metadata. Real photos can also be edited or exported without metadata. This inspector reports observable clues only; it does not assign a fake precision percentage or make a definitive authenticity claim. For high-stakes decisions, seek original files, provenance records, or a validated forensic review.
