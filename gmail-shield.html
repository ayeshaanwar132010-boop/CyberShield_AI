Gmail Shield

Connect your own Gmail account using a Google App Password where your Google account supports it. CyberShield checks recent messages and attachments locally on this Windows PC.

Google requirements: IMAP must be enabled in Gmail settings, and Google App Passwords require an eligible account with 2-Step Verification. Some accounts (including certain Workspace, Advanced Protection, or organization-managed accounts) may require OAuth instead. App Passwords do not bypass Google's security requirements.

Use a Google App Password generated in your Google Account security settings. Do not enter your normal Gmail password. The password is encrypted with Windows-protected Electron secure storage and is never displayed again after connecting. Do not share it with anyone.

Privacy: IMAP/SMTP connections use TLS. Message text and attachment contents are processed in memory by this app; attachments are not executed or uploaded to a scanning service. SMTP is verified during connection but CyberShield does not send email. This is a lightweight heuristic, not a full antivirus engine.
