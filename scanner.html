Privacy-first incident reporting

What to include in a report.

Share only what is needed to explain the incident. Do not upload private credentials or other people's personal data.

Useful evidence checklist

  • Date, time and time zone
  • Message text with secrets redacted
  • Suspicious URL as plain text (do not open it)
  • Sender name/number only if relevant
  • Screenshot with unrelated chats and notifications cropped
  • What happened and what action you took

Never include

Passwords, OTPs, PINs, recovery phrases, full payment-card/bank details, private photos, or unrelated people's messages. Redact them before sharing evidence. Use your country's official cybercrime reporting channel and verify the address independently.
